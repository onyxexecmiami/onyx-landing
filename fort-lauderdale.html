Who this serves

Fort Lauderdale bookings split three ways: airport transfers through FLL, cruise transfers to Port Everglades, and corporate or hotel travel around Las Olas Boulevard and downtown. The airport and the port are near each other but have separate entrances and traffic patterns, and some trips involve both — a flight arrival followed by a cruise departure, or the reverse — on different schedules with their own timing considerations.

The geography

I-95 runs the length of Broward County along the eastern side of the city, connecting Fort Lauderdale south to Miami-Dade and north toward Palm Beach County. I-595 branches east-west, feeding directly into FLL and Port Everglades from I-95 and Florida's Turnpike. Las Olas Boulevard, the city's downtown corridor, sits just north of the airport and port complex, closer to the beach than most visitors expect.

Airport access

Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International (FLL) is the local airport and the default choice for most trips originating here. Miami International (MIA) is a reasonable alternative to the south for specific routes or fares. President Donald J. Trump International Airport (DJT, formerly PBI) to the north is closer from Fort Lauderdale than from most of Miami-Dade, and worth considering for flights out of West Palm Beach.

Trip types

Cruise transfers to Port Everglades use flight tracking when the passenger is also arriving by air that day. Cruise check-in and departure times are fixed by the cruise line, not adjustable around a delayed flight, so arriving in Fort Lauderdale the day before a sailing is generally the lower-risk plan; a same-day flight-to-ship connection can work but carries the flight's own delay risk. Corporate travel around Las Olas and downtown Fort Lauderdale runs on the same accounts used for Miami-based firms with a Broward office or client.

A note on I-95 Express Lanes

The stretch of I-95 serving Fort Lauderdale includes tolled Express Lanes alongside the free general lanes. They can meaningfully change trip timing at peak hours, and using them is a routing decision made before dispatch, not an add-on discovered mid-trip.

Getting around Fort Lauderdale

  • I-95. The main north-south artery, connecting Fort Lauderdale to Miami-Dade to the south and Palm Beach County to the north.
  • I-595. The direct east-west link from I-95 and Florida's Turnpike into FLL and Port Everglades — the route most airport and cruise transfers use.
  • Airport and port, nearby but separate. FLL and Port Everglades sit close together but each has its own access and drop-off area — a same-day flight-to-ship connection is possible but depends on the flight landing on time.
  • Las Olas Boulevard. The downtown corridor sits north of the airport-port complex, on its own local street grid rather than directly off I-595.
  • Cruise turnaround days. Port Everglades sees heavy surrounding traffic on major embarkation and debarkation days, which is factored into pickup timing rather than treated as ordinary traffic.

Airport and port, one point of contact

An approved chauffeur and vehicle can be confirmed for both an FLL arrival and a Port Everglades departure on the same booking. Seating for up to six passengers, with luggage capacity for a flight-and-cruise itinerary confirmed before booking based on passenger count and bags.

Questions about Fort Lauderdale

Can you handle both my flight into FLL and my cruise from Port Everglades the same day?+
We can arrange both. Flight tracking is used for the airport leg, but cruise check-in and departure are fixed by the cruise line, not something we can move if the flight is delayed. Arriving the day before a sailing is the lower-risk plan; a same-day connection works when the flight lands on time.
Do you serve corporate travel around Las Olas and downtown Fort Lauderdale?+
Yes. Corporate accounts covering Fort Lauderdale offices run on the same terms as Miami-based accounts.
Is Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood always the best airport for a Fort Lauderdale trip?+
Usually, since it's the local option. Miami International or the Palm Beach airport (DJT, formerly PBI) can make sense for a specific route or fare.
How far is Port Everglades from FLL?+
The two sit close together in the same complex, connected by I-595 — a short, direct trip compared to most airport-to-port routes.
Do you cover trips between Fort Lauderdale and Miami-Dade or Palm Beach?+
Yes. I-95 connects Fort Lauderdale to both, and long-distance and hourly bookings across county lines are part of the service.
Can you meet a private flight into a Fort Lauderdale-area FBO?+
Yes. We track the tail number and coordinate with the FBO front desk, the same way as at the Miami-Dade FBOs — planeside or terminal pickup, whichever the FBO and your operation permit. See the Private Aviation page for detail.
