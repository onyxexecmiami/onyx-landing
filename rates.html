How the number gets built

Four things set your fare — nothing else.

  • Route. Distance and drive time between your pickup and drop-off, checked against real traffic conditions for your date, not a straight-line estimate.
  • Time of day. A 30% supplement applies between midnight and 6 a.m. — it's built into the quoted fare, never added after the ride.
  • Wait time beyond the complimentary window. 60 minutes are included at airports and FBOs; time held beyond that is the only variable added after booking, and it's agreed with you in advance, not discovered afterward.
  • The calendar. A handful of dates a year — Art Basel, major boat shows, F1, Ultra — put real pressure on vehicle availability across every operator in South Florida, ours included. Those dates are quoted individually, always before you confirm.

That's the entire list. No membership tiers, no surge multiplier, no "premium demand" language — just the four inputs above, quoted in writing before you decide.

Verify it yourself

What Permit #64666 actually verifies.

Miami-Dade County chauffeur registration isn't a badge we bought — it's a public record. Getting one requires a background check, a clean driving record on file with the county, and annual renewal to stay active. It's issued and tracked by Miami-Dade County's Department of Transportation and Public Works, independently of anything we say about ourselves.

Most operators in this market write "licensed and insured" without a number attached. We put ours in the footer of every page for one reason: it's checkable, and we'd rather you check it than take our word for it.

The comparison worth making

A confirmed fare vs. an app's estimate.

A rideshare estimate

  • The number on screen when you request is not the number when the car arrives — demand pricing can move it in either direction up to the moment of pickup.
  • The driver and vehicle are assigned at request time, from whoever is nearest and available.
  • Cancellations and driver reassignment are common in the minutes before pickup, especially at airports during peak arrival banks.

A confirmed fare

  • The number you're quoted is the number you pay, agreed before the vehicle is dispatched — not adjusted in transit.
  • The same vehicle and standard, held for your reservation specifically, not pulled from a shared pool.
  • One phone number if a flight changes or a meeting runs long — not a support ticket.

Neither model is wrong — they solve different problems. A rideshare app is built for availability at a moment's notice. A confirmed fare is built for a specific date you can't afford to leave to chance.

Policy, in detail

Cancellation, wait time, and the one exception.

How much complimentary wait time do I get?+
60 minutes at airports and FBOs, measured from actual landing, not the scheduled time. 15 minutes on hotel, residence, and other pickups.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 2 hours before pickup. We don't take prepayment, so a no-show carries no charge. A cancelled or heavily delayed flight is never charged.
Is there a supplement for late-night pickups?
Yes — a 30% supplement applies to pickups between midnight and 6 a.m. It's included in the fare you're quoted, not added afterward.
Are your rates per person or per vehicle?
Per vehicle. The fare is the same whether one passenger rides or the Suburban is full.
Where do you operate?
Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. See the service area page for specific neighborhoods and drive-time context.
