Arrivals at DJT / PBI

A smaller, easier-to-navigate airport than MIA or FLL, but the same standard applies: your chauffeur tracks the flight, adjusts to early or delayed arrivals, and meets you curbside or at a pre-arranged meet-and-greet point inside the terminal — confirmed at booking.

The airport was renamed in July 2026, and the airline booking code changed from PBI to DJT on August 18, 2026 — same location, same runways, same terminal, just a new official name and code. Search either DJT or PBI when booking your flight; nothing about the transfer itself is affected either way.

Covering the Palm Beach corridor

One operator covers DJT through Palm Beach, Boca Raton, and back down to Aventura and Miami-Dade, so a trip that starts at DJT and continues south stays with the same chauffeur and the same standard the whole way.

Fixed quote, confirmed first

Every DJT transfer is quoted at a fixed price before the trip, tolls and standard wait time included. No surge pricing, no reassigned driver mid-trip.
