Arrivals at DJT / PBI

A smaller, easier-to-navigate airport than MIA or FLL, but the same standard applies: your chauffeur tracks the flight, adjusts to early or delayed arrivals, and meets you curbside or at a pre-arranged meet-and-greet point inside the terminal — confirmed at booking.

The airport was renamed in July 2026, and the airline booking code changed from PBI to DJT on August 18, 2026 — same location, same runways, same terminal, just a new official name and code. Search either DJT or PBI when booking your flight; nothing about the transfer itself is affected either way.

Covering the Palm Beach corridor

One operator covers DJT through Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, and back down to Aventura and Miami-Dade, so a trip that starts at DJT and continues south stays with the same chauffeur and the same standard the whole way.

Golf clubs, extra luggage, or a full car for a group — capacity is confirmed at booking based on passenger count and what you're bringing, common for the golf and country-club traffic this airport sees.

Fixed quote, confirmed first

Every DJT transfer is quoted at a fixed price before the trip, tolls and standard wait time included. No surge pricing, no reassigned driver mid-trip.

Questions about DJT / PBI transfers

Do I book under the DJT code or the old PBI code?+
Either works for the transfer itself — same airport, same terminal, same runways. Search whichever code your airline or booking site still shows; it makes no difference to pickup.
Where at DJT / PBI do you meet arriving passengers?+
Curbside at arrivals by default, confirmed with you at booking. The terminal here is small enough that a meet-and-greet inside is rarely necessary, but it's available if you prefer it.
How far is DJT / PBI from Aventura and Miami-Dade?+
It's the longest of the three airports we cover — figure roughly 55–70 minutes down to Aventura outside peak traffic. One chauffeur covers the whole corridor, so a trip that starts here and continues south stays with the same driver.
Do you serve Palm Beach and Boca Raton directly from this airport?+
Yes. Palm Beach itself and Boca Raton are both short, routine runs from DJT — typically well under 30 minutes — well before continuing further south becomes relevant.
Is a private chauffeur worth it for a smaller airport like this?+
It's arguably more useful here than at MIA or FLL — rideshare and taxi availability at DJT can be thinner outside peak hours, and a pre-arranged pickup removes that uncertainty entirely.
