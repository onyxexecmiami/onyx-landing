Arrivals at PBI / DJT

A smaller, easier-to-navigate airport than MIA or FLL, but the same standard applies: your chauffeur tracks the flight, adjusts to early or delayed arrivals, and meets you curbside or inside at baggage claim — your choice at booking.

The airport itself was renamed in July 2026 — same location, same runways, same terminal, just a new official name and, from mid-August, a new airline booking code. Search PBI (or "West Palm Beach") until the code changes; nothing about the transfer itself is affected either way.

Covering the Palm Beach corridor

One operator covers PBI through Palm Beach, Boca Raton, and back down to Aventura and Miami-Dade, so a trip that starts at PBI and continues south stays with the same chauffeur and the same standard the whole way.

Fixed quote, confirmed first

Every PBI transfer is quoted at a fixed price before the trip, tolls and standard wait time included. No surge pricing, no reassigned driver mid-trip.
