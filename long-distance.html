Before You Book

How long-distance pricing works.

Is the vehicle shared with other passengers?+
No — the vehicle is yours for the trip, one party at a time, not a shared shuttle.
How many passengers and how much luggage fit?+
Up to six passengers; luggage capacity is confirmed at booking based on passenger count, bag count, and anything oversized like golf clubs or beach gear.
Can I book one-way, or does it have to be round-trip?+
Either. One-way is quoted with the return positioning built into the fare; round-trip with a wait or a separate pickup time is quoted the same way, as a fixed price.
What if I need the chauffeur to wait at the destination?+
Wait time — a few hours or a full day — is arranged at booking and priced into the quote, not billed as a surprise afterward.
Do you handle overnight or multi-day trips?+
Yes — for multi-day itineraries, lodging and scheduling for the chauffeur are coordinated as part of the quote.
