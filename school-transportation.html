Setting Up a Family Route

What we confirm before the first pickup.

What if the primary chauffeur is unavailable one day?+
An approved backup chauffeur, known to the family in advance, covers the route — never an unannounced substitute showing up cold.
Who is authorized to change pickup times or details?+
Only the parents, guardians, or family office contacts named when the route is set up. Changes from anyone else are confirmed with an authorized contact before they're made.
What information do you need about the child?+
Age, height, and weight, to arrange the correct child seat or booster — plus an emergency contact on file separate from the day-to-day authorized contacts.
How is drop-off confirmed?+
The chauffeur confirms arrival at school and a completed handoff — the child is not left at a drop point unconfirmed.
Can I set this up weekly, monthly, or for the school term?+
Yes — routes are typically arranged by the week, month, or full term, whichever matches the family's schedule.
What is the cancellation policy for a single day?+
Tell your authorized contact as early as possible; standing routes are held for the arranged schedule, and single-day changes are handled directly, not through a general cancellation form.
Is our information kept private?+
Yes — route details, schedules, and any information about the children are shared only with the assigned chauffeur and are not discussed outside the arrangement.
