Account features

What an account includes.

  • Consolidated billing. One statement, clean records for expense reporting.
  • Authorized bookers. Executive assistants and travel coordinators can book and confirm rides directly on the account, without going through the traveler.
  • Ride confirmations. Each booking is confirmed back in writing — vehicle, time, and pickup point — before the day of travel.
  • Saved preferences. Greeting, route, temperature, water, charging — on file per traveler.
  • Cost centers. Trips can be tagged by department or project for internal billing, on request.
  • Direct line. Reach the person responsible, not a call center.
  • COI & W-9 on request. Provided when your office, venue, or procurement process requires it.
  • Discretion. Unmarked vehicle, no detail repeated outside the booking.

Corporate FAQ

Questions from travel teams

Who can book on a corporate account?+
Any authorized booker you name — an executive assistant, office manager, or travel coordinator — can book and confirm rides directly, without routing every request through the traveler.
How does billing work?+
One consolidated statement for the account, with trips tagged by department or project on request for internal cost-center tracking.
Can you provide a COI or W-9 before we book?+
Yes — both are provided on request, typically before the first booking clears if your office or venue requires it as part of vendor setup.
Do you offer NET terms?+
Billing terms are set up individually per account when it's opened — ask when you reach out and we'll confirm what fits.
What happens if a traveler's plans change last minute?+
Changes go to the same direct line that confirmed the booking — not a queue — and are updated on the ride in real time.
