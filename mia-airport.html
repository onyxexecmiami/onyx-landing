Arrivals at MIA

MIA's terminal layout and pickup zones change by airline and concourse. Your flight is tracked from wheels-down, and your chauffeur confirms pickup once you're through the process for your arrival type:

  • Domestic arrivals — met curbside at baggage claim, typically the faster of the two.
  • International arrivals — met past customs and baggage; the extra processing time is tracked and built into the pickup plan, not treated as a delay.

To Aventura, Brickell & PortMiami

MIA–Aventura/Sunny Isles typically runs 30-40 minutes outside peak hours. MIA–Brickell and Downtown run closer to 20-25 minutes. MIA–PortMiami for a same-day cruise connection runs roughly 15-20 minutes. Exact timing confirmed at booking based on your destination.

Questions about MIA transfers

Where exactly at MIA do you meet arriving passengers?+
Curbside at the arrivals level by default, or a meet-and-greet point just past baggage claim if you'd rather not manage bags to the curb alone — confirmed with you when you book, not decided on the day.
What happens if my flight is delayed or lands early?+
Your chauffeur tracks the flight in real time and adjusts the pickup window automatically. Early or late, the fixed quote you were given at booking doesn't change.
Do you serve MIA for connecting cruise passengers?+
Yes. MIA-to-PortMiami and MIA-to-Port Everglades transfers are booked the same way as any airport run, timed against your ship's boarding window rather than a generic drive-time estimate.
Is there room for golf clubs or extra luggage from MIA?+
The Suburban RST seats up to six with luggage capacity confirmed before booking based on passenger count, bag count, and anything oversized like golf clubs or strollers.
Can I book a one-way MIA transfer and a separate return later?+
Yes. Most bookings are one-way pickups or drop-offs; round-trip and standing return dates are arranged just as easily on the same fixed-quote basis.
