Multi-Vehicle & Groups

How a coordinated event day runs.

How does staging work for a group arrival?+
Vehicles are staged ahead of the call time at a confirmed point near the venue, sequenced so the group arrives together rather than trickling in.
Is there a single point of contact on the day?+
Yes — one day-of contact coordinates all vehicles and timing, so you're not managing multiple drivers separately if plans shift.
Do you need a passenger manifest in advance?+
For multi-vehicle bookings, yes — names and vehicle assignments confirmed ahead of the event avoid confusion at pickup, especially for weddings and large parties.
What about the return trip at the end of the night?+
Return timing is planned at booking, not left open-ended — whether that's a fixed pickup time or an on-call window once the event wraps.
Who drives if you need more vehicles than you have?+
Onyx's own vehicle is a privately maintained 2026 Chevrolet Suburban RST. When an event needs more vehicles than that, a vetted partner vehicle is arranged and confirmed with you before booking — never a surprise on the day.
