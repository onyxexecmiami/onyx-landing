Where to Eat in Sunny Isles Beach

From oceanfront Italian and Greek seafood to neighborhood sushi, charcoal-grilled Mediterranean food and a steak dinner that can continue well past midnight — Sunny Isles Beach has more variety than its two-mile stretch of Collins Avenue might suggest.

The neighborhood

More than hotel dining.

Sunny Isles Beach is narrow enough that nearly everything revolves around Collins Avenue, yet its restaurant scene covers several very different kinds of evenings.

You can book a formal oceanfront dinner, sit down for neighborhood Italian, order sushi a few blocks from the beach, stop for charcoal-grilled kebabs or begin with dinner and stay for music late into the night.

That variety is also why there is no useful single answer to "what is the best restaurant in Sunny Isles?" The better question is what kind of evening you are planning.

How we organized this guide

Choose the restaurant for the occasion.

This is not a numerical ranking. A restaurant that works for an anniversary dinner is not necessarily where you want lunch after the beach, and a traditional steakhouse does not solve the same problem as dinner with live music afterward.

So the ONYX guide groups Sunny Isles restaurants by what they are actually useful for: a full evening, special occasions, neighborhood dining, sushi, casual meals and late nights.

Dinner that can become a night out

Porterhouse Music Bar & Restaurant

17004 Collins Avenue

Porterhouse makes sense when dinner is only the beginning of the evening. It starts as a steak-focused restaurant with a full dinner service, but the identity changes as the night moves on.

On Friday and Saturday, the restaurant schedules live singers during dinner and DJs later in the evening, with the lounge continuing until early morning. That makes Porterhouse different from a conventional steakhouse: you do not necessarily need to decide where to go after dinner.

For a group celebrating a birthday, meeting friends, having cocktails after dinner or simply wanting an evening that does not end with dessert, it is one of the more distinctive formats in Sunny Isles.

When we'd choose it

Dinner + drinks + music.

Choose Porterhouse when your priority is not simply "the best steak." Choose it when you want one address to carry a larger portion of the evening.

The dinner service runs until midnight. The bar and lounge continue later, with extended weekend hours.

That also makes it useful when you are hosting friends or visitors and do not want to plan three separate venues for dinner, cocktails and music.

Best for: date night, celebrations, dinner with friends, steak and a late evening.

Italian · Special Occasion

Il Mulino New York

17875 Collins Avenue · Acqualina Resort

Il Mulino is the more traditional special-occasion choice in this guide. The Sunny Isles location brings the New York Italian restaurant to an oceanfront setting inside Acqualina, with both indoor and terrace dining.

It serves lunch and dinner daily, which also makes it more useful than many formal restaurants if you are arranging a business lunch or an earlier afternoon meal rather than an evening reservation.

This is where we would look for a polished Italian dinner, a client meal, anniversary or an occasion where the setting and service matter as much as what happens after dinner.

Best for: special occasions, Italian, business lunch, couples and hotel guests.

Greek · Seafood

Avra Miami

17945 Collins Avenue

Avra takes the evening in a different direction: Greek and Mediterranean cooking with seafood at the center of the concept.

The restaurant is part of the Acqualina dining collection and operates in an indoor-outdoor setting near the ocean.

If one person in the group wants a formal evening but another does not want another heavy steakhouse meal, Avra is an obvious alternative.

Best for: seafood, Greek-Mediterranean cuisine, special occasions and dinner near Acqualina.

The neighborhood institution

Timo

17624 Collins Avenue

Timo has been part of Sunny Isles since 2003, which is meaningful in an area where restaurants and hotels change names regularly.

Chef Tim Andriola's menu is Italian-Mediterranean and changes seasonally, while the restaurant deliberately maintains the feel of a neighborhood dining room rather than a hotel restaurant.

It works especially well when you want a proper dinner without turning the night into an event.

Best for: neighborhood Italian, couples, quiet dinner and returning local guests.

Italian · Local

H2O Cafe

18108 Collins Avenue

H2O describes itself as an Italian restaurant with cross-cultural Mediterranean influences. It is smaller and more relaxed than the large resort dining rooms further south on Collins Avenue.

That makes it useful for families or visitors looking for Italian food without making dinner the central production of the entire evening.

Best for: Italian, family dinner and a quieter local option.

Japanese & Thai

When you want sushi instead

Tako Sushi

17630 Collins Avenue

Family-owned and operated, Tako serves both Japanese and Thai food. It is the straightforward neighborhood choice for sushi, rolls and Thai dishes without the atmosphere or pricing structure of a resort restaurant.

Best for: casual sushi, families and takeout.

Rice Asian House & Sushi Bar

17100 Collins Avenue

Rice Asian House has served Sunny Isles since 2008 and combines sushi with a substantial Thai menu. It is another practical local restaurant rather than a destination dining room.

Best for: sushi, Thai food, delivery and casual dinner.

Neither restaurant needs to pretend to be a Miami Beach omakase destination. Their value is exactly the opposite: they are reliable neighborhood choices when you want sushi five minutes from your building.

Mediterranean · Casual

Mangal Mediterranean Grill

17066 Collins Avenue

Mangal centers the menu around charcoal grilling: shawarma, kebabs and Mediterranean plates cooked over live coals.

This is one of the easiest restaurants in the guide to use spontaneously. It works for lunch, an uncomplicated family dinner or takeout after a day at the beach.

Best for: kebabs, shawarma, casual lunch and family meals.

Azerbaijani · International

Baku Cafe

17080 Collins Avenue

Sunny Isles has always had a more international dining identity than many Miami neighborhoods, and Baku Cafe is a good example.

The restaurant draws on Azerbaijani cooking, with grilled meats and Caucasus influences, and operates from morning through late evening.

It is particularly useful for groups who want something outside the predictable Italian-steak-sushi rotation.

Best for: Azerbaijani cuisine, grilled meats, groups and an international menu.

Casual · Beach Day

La Sandwicherie

17008 Collins Avenue

Not every meal in Sunny Isles needs a reservation. La Sandwicherie brings the long-running Miami French sandwich-bar concept to Collins Avenue and fills the gap between "restaurant dinner" and simply needing something good to eat.

Sandwiches, salads and a casual format make it particularly useful before or after the beach, for lunch or when a formal dining room is exactly what you do not want.

Best for: breakfast, lunch, casual food, takeout and beach days.

The point

Not every restaurant has to be a destination.

One of the mistakes visitors make in South Florida is treating every meal as another reservation to research.

If you already have Il Mulino or Avra booked for dinner, there is nothing wrong with keeping lunch simple. A useful neighborhood guide should tell you where to celebrate and where simply to eat.

From Sunny Isles

Dinner doesn't have to stay in the neighborhood.

Sunny Isles also sits between several of South Florida's main dining areas. Aventura is immediately south, Bal Harbour and Surfside continue down Collins Avenue, while Miami Beach, the Design District and Fort Lauderdale are natural choices when the evening calls for a different neighborhood.

FAQ

Sunny Isles Beach restaurants

Where should I go for a special dinner in Sunny Isles Beach?+
Il Mulino New York and Avra are two of the more formal choices around Acqualina. Porterhouse is a better fit when you want the evening to continue into music and drinks afterward.
Where should I go for steak and a late night?+
Porterhouse combines its dinner service with a late-night bar and lounge format. On Friday and Saturday, its published schedule includes live singers during dinner and DJs later at night.
What are good local Italian restaurants?+
Timo is the long-running neighborhood option, while H2O Cafe offers another Italian-Mediterranean restaurant along Collins Avenue. For a more formal oceanfront experience, Il Mulino is the stronger special-occasion choice.
Where can I get sushi in Sunny Isles?+
Tako Sushi and Rice Asian House both offer Japanese and Thai menus and work well as casual neighborhood restaurants.
Where can I eat casually after the beach?+
Mangal is useful for Mediterranean grilled food, while La Sandwicherie is the simplest option for sandwiches, salads and an uncomplicated casual meal.
Do I need a car to go to dinner in Sunny Isles?+
Many restaurants are concentrated along Collins Avenue, so whether you need transportation depends on where you are staying and what the rest of the evening looks like. If dinner is followed by another stop outside Sunny Isles, pre-arranged transportation or an hourly chauffeur can simplify a multi-stop evening.
