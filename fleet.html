Questions about the vehicle

Why only one vehicle instead of a larger fleet?+
One vehicle, maintained to a single standard, means you know exactly what's arriving before it does. As Onyx grows to a small network, every added vehicle is held to the same standard rather than rotated through a rental pool.
Is the vehicle cleaned between every trip?+
Yes. Interior and exterior are checked and cleaned between trips, not on a fixed schedule that could leave it presentable-but-not-immaculate for a same-day back-to-back booking.
Can the Suburban fit golf clubs, strollers, or oversized luggage?+
Usually, yes — capacity depends on passenger count alongside bag count and size. Tell us what you're bringing at booking and we'll confirm it fits before you arrive, not after.
What happens if the vehicle needs unexpected maintenance?+
Maintenance is scheduled around bookings, not the other way around, and the network of vetted partner vehicles covers the rare case of an unplanned issue so a booking is never simply cancelled.
Are child seats included, or do I need to bring my own?+
Child seats are arranged in advance once you tell us ages at booking — you don't need to bring your own unless you specifically prefer to.
