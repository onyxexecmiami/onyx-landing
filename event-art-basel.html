Miami Art Week 2026

One week. Multiple destinations.

Art Basel Miami Beach's public days run Friday, December 4 through Sunday, December 6, 2026, with VIP Days on December 2–3 at the Miami Beach Convention Center.

Miami Art Week activity begins earlier in the week. Design Miami runs December 1–6, while galleries, private events, exhibitions and satellite programming take place throughout Miami Beach, Wynwood and the Miami Design District.

For visitors attending more than one event, transportation is rarely a single trip. The schedule may move from an airport or FBO to a hotel, from the hotel to the fair, across the bay for a gallery opening, then back to Miami Beach for dinner or an evening event.

The transportation plan

Traffic is the variable. Your transportation doesn't have to be.

The distances between Miami Beach, Wynwood and the Miami Design District can look short on a map. During a major event week, however, the time between appointments matters more than the mileage. The Convention Center itself isn't served by Metrorail, and on-site parking is limited and fills early during the fair.

A pre-arranged chauffeur gives you one transportation plan for the day instead of arranging each ride separately. Pickup times, destinations and return plans can be coordinated before the day begins and adjusted as your schedule develops.

Choose the arrangement

One chauffeur, one plan.

Build the day around your Art Week schedule rather than individual rides. This works particularly well for days that combine Art Basel, Design Miami, gallery visits, Wynwood, dinner reservations and evening events.

  • Point-to-point. Pre-arranged transportation between MIA, FLL, private aviation terminals, Miami Beach hotels and your first Art Week destination.
  • Hourly, dedicated chauffeur. Reserve the vehicle for a multi-stop schedule covering fairs, galleries, restaurants, hotels and evening events.
  • Multi-day. Coordinate several days under one transportation plan for visitors, collectors, executives, families or small groups.
