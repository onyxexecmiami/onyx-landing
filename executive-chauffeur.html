How Hourly Works

The details, before you book.

Is there a minimum booking length?+
Yes — minimum hours are confirmed with your quote, based on your date and itinerary. There's no single published number because it varies by day and demand.
What if I need extra time beyond what I booked?+
Additional time is billed by the hour at the same rate, subject to the chauffeur's availability for the rest of the day — confirm in advance if there's a chance you'll run long.
Can I add stops that weren't in the original plan?+
Yes, within the hours you've booked. Extra stops don't require a new booking — just tell your chauffeur.
What does "as-directed" mean?+
The chauffeur follows your instructions in real time rather than a fixed route — wait here, go there next, change plans mid-afternoon. You're not locked into an itinerary set at booking.
Are parking and tolls included?+
Tolls are included in the quoted rate. Parking or valet fees at a specific venue, if any, are confirmed case by case.
What happens during standby or a meal break?+
Standby time — waiting while you're in a meeting or at dinner — is part of the hours you've booked. Your chauffeur takes a meal break during long standby periods without leaving you without coverage; this is coordinated so it never falls during an active pickup.
Is there room for luggage or shopping bags between stops?+
Yes — capacity is confirmed at booking based on what you're bringing, common for shopping trips or a day that starts or ends at the airport.
Can I extend on the day, once we're already out?+
Usually, yes, but it's subject to availability — if the chauffeur has another booking after yours, an extension may not be possible. Flagging a likely extension in advance avoids that.
