Arrivals at FLL

Your chauffeur tracks the flight and adjusts to early or delayed arrivals, then meets you curbside or at a pre-arranged meet-and-greet point inside the terminal — confirmed at booking, by your specific terminal (FLL runs Terminals 1 through 4, each with its own curb). A reasonable buffer for customs delays is factored into the pickup plan — ask us to confirm current wait-time terms when you book.

To Aventura, Sunny Isles, Las Olas & South Florida

FLL to the Aventura/Sunny Isles corridor typically runs 25-35 minutes outside peak hours — this is close to our home base, so timing and routing are well established, not a guess based on a map app. FLL to Las Olas and downtown Fort Lauderdale is closer still, typically 10-15 minutes.

Fixed quote, confirmed first

Every FLL transfer is quoted at a fixed price before the trip, tolls and standard wait time included. No surge pricing, no reassigned driver mid-trip.

Cruise connections

Continuing on a cruise from Fort Lauderdale? FLL sits minutes from Port Everglades, and we handle cruise-sized luggage as a matter of course. Flying in the day before your sailing rather than same-day removes the single biggest source of missed-boarding risk — worth considering for any cruise with a fixed departure time. Questions about FLL transfers

Where at FLL do you meet arriving passengers?+
Curbside at arrivals by default, or a meet-and-greet point past baggage claim if you'd rather not manage bags to the curb alone — confirmed with you when you book.
How does Onyx handle a delayed or early FLL landing?+
Your chauffeur tracks the flight in real time and adjusts the pickup window automatically, at no change to the fixed quote you were given at booking.
Do you cover Port Everglades cruise transfers from FLL?+
Yes. FLL-to-Port Everglades is a short, well-established run for us given how close the airport sits to the port, timed against your ship's boarding window.
Is FLL usually faster than MIA for South Florida arrivals?+
For the Aventura/Sunny Isles corridor, yes in most cases — FLL tends to run 25–35 minutes versus MIA's 25–40, and FLL generally sees less airport-access congestion. Fort Lauderdale and Boca Raton destinations naturally favor FLL further still.
Can I book a standing weekly or seasonal FLL pickup?+
Yes. Recurring FLL pickups for seasonal residents or frequent business travelers are arranged the same way as a one-off transfer, with the same chauffeur held for the standing schedule where possible.
