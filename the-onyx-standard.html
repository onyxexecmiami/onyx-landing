The Onyx Standard

Built behind the wheel. Held the same at every mile.

Onyx Executive Miami is a boutique chauffeur company, not a marketplace and not a franchise. One founder set the standard by driving it — and personally vets every chauffeur who carries it forward.

Why one standard matters.

Large networks route you to whichever vehicle is nearest. The result is uneven: a different stranger each time, a call center between you and the person driving. Onyx is built the other way around. The founder began by driving every trip, and as the network grows, holds each chauffeur to the same standard of safety, discretion, and presentation.

You work with one accountable partner — whoever is assigned, the standard is the same, and one person stands behind it.
