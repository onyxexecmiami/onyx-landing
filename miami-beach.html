Three causeways, one decision each time

MacArthur, Julia Tuttle, and Venetian each carry different traffic at different hours — the fastest route to MIA from Ocean Drive at 4 p.m. is rarely the fastest one at 9 a.m. That call gets made before you're in the vehicle, not from the back seat.

Airport & FBO transfers

Flight-tracked pickups to Miami International and Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood, and planeside FBO transfers timed to your tail number. Budget extra buffer on event weekends — see below.

Hotel & residence pickups

Beachfront hotel porte-cochères each run their own valet choreography, and Fisher Island adds a ferry crossing most services don't plan for. We know which entrance, which lane, and how much lead time each one actually needs.

Event-day routing

Art Basel, Miami Swim Week, and the South Beach Wine & Food Festival close streets and back up every causeway at once. On those dates, pickup timing is built around the closures, not against them — see our Art Basel page for specifics on that week.
