Who this serves

Miami Beach covers three distinct stretches — South Beach, Mid-Beach, and North Beach — and Onyx treats them as three different jobs rather than one address. Hotel guests and residents in each stretch are reached by a different causeway, and event-day traffic hits the causeways unevenly depending on which fair, game, or show is drawing crowds that week.

The geography

South Beach sits at the southern tip, reached most directly by the MacArthur Causeway from Downtown Miami. Mid-Beach, centered around 41st Street, is reached by the Julia Tuttle Causeway, which runs closest to I-95. North Beach connects via the 79th Street Causeway. The Broad Causeway is a separate crossing further north that lands in Bay Harbor Islands, not directly in Miami Beach — reaching North Beach from that direction means continuing south through Surfside and Bal Harbour on local streets.

Airport access

Miami International (MIA) is the standard choice for most of Miami Beach. The Julia Tuttle Causeway and I-95 form the most direct route from Mid-Beach; from South Beach, the route depends on the exact address and traffic and may run via the MacArthur Causeway and I-395 instead. Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood (FLL) is a longer trip north, more relevant for North Beach pickups than South Beach ones. President Donald J. Trump International Airport (DJT, formerly PBI) is a longer trip still, reserved for a specific route.

Trip types

Hotel arrivals and departures across all three stretches, event transportation for galas, conferences, and fairs, and airport transfers make up most bookings. During Art Basel and Miami Art Week, fair-to-fair and hotel-to-fair trips add a layer of scheduling that a single causeway crossing doesn't capture — see the Art Basel page for that detail.

A note on Fisher Island

Fisher Island, off the southern tip of Miami Beach, is reached by a separate car ferry rather than any causeway. A trip there would need to be coordinated with the ferry schedule rather than treated as a standard Miami Beach pickup.

Which causeway, and why it matters

  • MacArthur Causeway. The direct route from Downtown Miami to South Beach — the one that matters most for Ocean Drive and Fifth Street addresses.
  • Julia Tuttle Causeway. Runs closest to I-95 and is the main route to Mid-Beach and the 41st Street corridor — also the most direct path to and from MIA.
  • 79th Street Causeway. Serves North Beach directly. The Broad Causeway, further north, lands in Bay Harbor Islands and reaches North Beach only via local streets through Surfside and Bal Harbour.
  • Events back up the causeways. Art Basel, conventions, and large games slow the MacArthur and Tuttle in particular — timing a pickup around the event, not just the address, matters here.
  • Venetian Causeway. A smaller, quieter toll route connecting the Arts & Entertainment District to the Venetian Islands and Mid-Beach, useful as an alternate when the Tuttle is backed up.

The right causeway, not a guess

An approved chauffeur and vehicle can be confirmed for a South Beach hotel arrival in the morning and a Mid-Beach dinner run that evening, routed by whichever causeway is actually moving. Questions about Miami Beach

Which causeway do you use to reach Miami Beach?+
Whichever is fastest for your specific pickup and time — typically MacArthur for South Beach, Julia Tuttle for Mid-Beach, and the 79th Street Causeway for North Beach, though the exact route depends on traffic and the specific address.
Do you serve all three parts of Miami Beach — South, Mid, and North?+
Yes. Each is booked and routed as its own trip rather than a single generic 'Miami Beach' pickup.
Is Miami International the right airport for a Miami Beach hotel?+
Generally yes for Miami Beach as a whole, though the route varies: Mid-Beach usually runs via the Julia Tuttle Causeway, while South Beach may use the MacArthur Causeway and I-395 instead, depending on traffic. Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood becomes more relevant the further north you are.
Do you handle Art Basel and Art Week transportation?+
Yes — see the dedicated Art Basel Miami Beach page for fair-to-fair and hotel-to-fair scheduling during Art Week.
How do you handle traffic during a major event or convention?+
By building the event into the pickup time rather than treating the causeways as a fixed drive — event-day timing is checked before dispatch, not assumed.
Can you arrange transportation to Fisher Island?+
Fisher Island is reached only by a car ferry, not any causeway. This would need to be confirmed as a special arrangement rather than booked as a standard Miami Beach pickup — contact us to check availability.
