PortMiami

Multiple cruise terminals along the same port road, each with its own drop sequence on a turnaround morning. From Brickell or Downtown, the approach over the MacArthur Causeway is a different drive on a ship's departure day than on any other — timed accordingly, not guessed at.

Port Everglades

Fort Lauderdale's port draws heavy traffic from FLL arrivals stacking with cruise embarkation on the same mornings. Terminal assignment and luggage handling are confirmed before pickup, not sorted out curbside.

What's included

Door to terminal, luggage handled.

  • Terminal-aware drop-off. The right terminal on the right road, not a generic "PortMiami" pin.
  • Luggage handling. From the vehicle to the terminal curb.
  • Fixed fare, confirmed before pickup. No adjustment for turnaround-day traffic.
  • Return transfers. Same standard on disembarkation day, coordinated around your ship's actual clearance time.
